## Limits of review

The evidence review did not reconstruct features or covariance fits from raw
source data. Root's completed strict verification covers full DEV score
reproduction and model algebra; the reviewer independently checks the retained
numeric evidence. Whole-archive authenticity is delegated to the previous
official-source audit rather than reading TEST bytes to recompute it here.
Tests were read through retained logs in this concrete review, not rerun;
timings were checked, not independently remeasured. No population-FAR,
independent-confirmation or deployment conclusion follows.
